{% comment %} Start-up animation, at the top of app.html's . When the home screen app starts, iOS shows a launch image (PWA_APP_SPLASH_SCREEN in settings): the LUT logo, PWA_SPLASH_LOGO_WIDTH (128) px wide, in the middle of the screen on PWA_APP_BACKGROUND_COLOR. This covers the page with the same picture, so the change from the launch image to the page is not seen, and when the page has loaded, the logo shrinks a little, then zooms past the viewer while the cover fades away. It plays once each time the app starts: not in a browser tab, nor on later pages (Turbo runs this again on every page, and form posts load whole pages). {% endcomment %}
