About LUT Kyyti


Who can use LUT Kyyti?

LUT Kyyti is intended for LUT and LAB employees. Access is limited to users with a verified LUT or LAB email address.

During the pilot phase, the service is not available to the general public.


How does LUT Kyyti work?

Drivers post rides they are already planning to make. Passengers can search for suitable rides and request to join them.

Passengers can also post ride requests when they are looking for a ride. The driver and passenger then agree on practical details such as the exact pickup point and timing.


Is LUT Kyyti a taxi or transport service?

No. LUT Kyyti is not a taxi, bus, or transport provider.

It is a platform that helps members of the LUT and LAB community find other people travelling in the same direction. LUT Kyyti does not operate vehicles or employ drivers.


Does LUT Kyyti plan routes for me?

No. LUT Kyyti does not provide route planning.

Drivers and passengers enter their own trip details, including the departure area, destination, date, and time.


Can students use LUT Kyyti?

During the pilot phase, LUT Kyyti is available only to LUT and LAB employees.

The service may later be opened to LUT and LAB students after the pilot has been successfully completed and evaluated.


Can I use LUT Kyyti for trips other than commuting?

The main purpose of LUT Kyyti is to support commuting to and from LUT and LAB campuses and workplaces.

Other suitable trips may also be allowed, depending on the service rules during the pilot.


Why should I use LUT Kyyti?

LUT Kyyti can help reduce commuting costs, make everyday travel more social, and lower transport-related emissions by filling empty seats in vehicles already making the journey.

You can learn more on the Impact page.


Accounts and privacy


Is my LUT/LAB account connected to my LUT Kyyti account?

No. Your LUT Kyyti account is separate from your LUT or LAB user account.

You register using your LUT or LAB email address and create a separate password for LUT Kyyti.


Can I use my LUT/LAB account password for LUT Kyyti?

You should not reuse your LUT or LAB account password.

Create a separate and unique password for LUT Kyyti. LUT Kyyti will never ask for your LUT or LAB account password.


How is my LUT/LAB email address verified?

When registering, you will be asked to verify your LUT or LAB email address.

Verification confirms that you are eligible to use the service. Follow the instructions sent to your email address to complete the registration process.


What should I do if I forget my password?

Use the password reset option on the login page.

A password reset message will be sent to the email address connected to your LUT Kyyti account.


Do I need to provide my home address?

No. LUT Kyyti does not ask you to provide your home address.

When posting a trip, you can enter a city, town, neighbourhood, campus, or another suitable meeting area as the departure point. You and the other rider can agree on the exact pickup location separately.


What information can other users see about me?

Other users may see limited information needed to arrange a trip, such as your first name and relevant trip details.

Your contact information is shared according to the contact preferences you select and the way the service is configured. Exact visibility rules should be explained in the Privacy Policy.


How can I communicate with another rider?

When creating your account, you may be asked to provide contact information such as your email address and phone number.

You can choose which available contact methods other riders may use. Drivers and passengers are responsible for agreeing on practical details directly with each other.


Can I delete my LUT Kyyti account?

Yes. You can request the deletion of your LUT Kyyti account and associated personal information.

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period where required by law or for legitimate administrative, security, or dispute-resolution purposes. Further information is available in the Privacy Policy.


What happens to my account if I leave LUT or LAB?

LUT Kyyti is intended for current LUT and LAB community members.

If you no longer have an eligible LUT or LAB email address or employment relationship, your access may be restricted or your account may be closed in accordance with the Terms of Service.


Posting and joining trips


Can I offer a recurring ride?

Yes. If you regularly commute on the same route, you can post a recurring ride so others can join when suitable.

Make sure the days, times, and recurrence details are accurate and update the ride if your schedule changes.


Can I join a ride as a passenger?

Yes. You can browse available rides and request to join a ride that matches your travel needs.

A ride is not necessarily confirmed until the driver accepts your request.


Can I post a ride request?

Yes. If you cannot find a suitable posted ride, you can create a ride request describing where and when you would like to travel.

Other users can then respond if they are able to offer a suitable ride.


Can I look for travel companions for train, bus, or taxi trips?

Yes. You can also post a trip if you are looking for other people to share public transport or taxi travel.

The users are responsible for making the booking and agreeing on any costs directly with each other.


Does a driver have to accept every passenger request?

No. Drivers can decide whether to accept or decline a passenger request.

They may consider factors such as available seats, pickup arrangements, timing, and whether the requested trip fits their planned route.


How many passengers can join a ride?

The driver decides how many seats are available and enters this information when posting the ride.

The number of accepted passengers should not exceed the available seating capacity of the vehicle.


What happens after I request to join a ride?

The driver reviews the request and can accept or decline it.

Once the request is accepted, the driver and passenger can agree on the exact pickup point, timing, and any other practical arrangements.


How will I know if someone requests to join my ride?

LUT Kyyti will notify you through the notification methods available in the service.

You should also check your account regularly for new requests and changes to existing trips.


Who decides the pickup and drop-off points?

The driver sets the main trip details when posting the ride.

The driver and passenger can then agree on suitable pickup and drop-off arrangements. Neither person is required to share their home address.


Can I edit or delete a trip after posting it?

Yes. You can update or remove a trip if your plans change.

If another user has already requested or agreed to join the trip, inform them as early as possible about any changes or cancellation.


Can I cancel a ride or passenger request?

Yes. Plans can change, and both drivers and passengers may cancel.

Cancel as early as possible and inform the other person so they have time to make alternative arrangements.


What if the driver or passenger is late?

Drivers and passengers are responsible for communicating with each other.

Agree on the timing clearly in advance and contact the other person as soon as possible if you expect to be late.


Costs and payments


Is using LUT Kyyti free?

Yes. Using the LUT Kyyti platform is free.

Any cost sharing between the driver and passenger, such as a contribution towards fuel costs, is agreed directly between the users.


Does LUT Kyyti handle payments?

No. LUT Kyyti does not process or collect payments.

Drivers and passengers are responsible for agreeing on any possible cost sharing and arranging payment directly with each other.


How should ride costs be shared?

Any contribution should be agreed before the trip.

The purpose of LUT Kyyti is to support shared travel, not commercial passenger transport. Drivers are responsible for ensuring that any cost-sharing arrangement complies with applicable laws, insurance terms, and tax rules.


Safety and responsibilities


Is LUT Kyyti safe to use?

LUT Kyyti is intended for verified LUT and LAB community members, which makes it different from an open public marketplace.

However, verification does not guarantee another user’s behaviour, driving ability, identity beyond the information verified by the service, or the safety of a particular trip. Users should use normal care when arranging and sharing rides.


Who is responsible for the vehicle and the journey?

The driver is responsible for the vehicle, driving licence, insurance, roadworthiness, legal compliance, and safe operation of the vehicle.

Passengers are responsible for behaving appropriately and following reasonable safety instructions. LUT Kyyti only helps users find and contact each other.


Does LUT Kyyti provide insurance?

No additional vehicle, passenger, accident, or travel insurance is provided by LUT Kyyti unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Drivers should ensure that their vehicle insurance covers the planned journey and any permitted cost-sharing arrangement.


What should I do if I feel uncomfortable about a ride?

Do not join or continue with a ride if you feel uncomfortable or believe the situation may be unsafe.

You may cancel the arrangement, leave the ride when it is safe to do so, and contact the LUT Kyyti team to report your concern.

In an emergency, contact the appropriate emergency services.


What should I do if another user does not arrive?

Try to contact the person using the agreed contact method.

If they do not respond, make alternative travel arrangements where possible. Repeated no-shows or misuse of the service can be reported to the LUT Kyyti team.


Can I report inappropriate behaviour or misuse?

Yes. You should report harassment, threatening behaviour, unsafe driving, misleading trip information, repeated no-shows, misuse of personal information, or other violations of the service rules.

Reports can be submitted through the contact or reporting method provided by LUT Kyyti.


Can LUT Kyyti suspend or remove a user account?

Yes. Access may be restricted, suspended, or removed if a user violates the Terms of Service, misuses the platform, provides misleading information, or creates safety or security concerns.

Any action will be handled in accordance with the Terms of Service and applicable university procedures.


Help and support


Where can I find instructions for using LUT Kyyti?

Detailed instructions for using account features, posting trips, joining rides, managing requests, and changing settings are available on the Help page after you log in.


Who can I contact for help or feedback?

You can contact the LUT Kyyti team through the Contact page.

Questions, feedback, problem reports, and improvement ideas are welcome, especially during the pilot phase.
