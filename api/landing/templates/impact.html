{% trans "Why LUT Kyyti?" %}

LUT University aims to reach net zero by 2050 and is working systematically to reduce the climate impact of its operations. One important part of this work is everyday mobility: how staff and students travel to and from campus.


Staff and student commuting is one of the largest sources of LUT University’s carbon footprint, accounting for around a third of total emissions. LUT’s Climate Action Plan 2025–2030 recognises commuting as a key area for action and includes measures to support more sustainable travel choices, such as cooperation with campus cities and transport providers, incentives for sustainable commuting, parking management, cycling support, and awareness-raising.


LUT Kyyti is one practical step in this direction. The service helps LUT and LAB community members find others travelling in the same direction, so that existing car journeys can be shared instead of made alone. While the long-term electrification of private commuting will reduce emissions, sharing rides can already help make current commuting more efficient by filling empty seats in cars that are already making the trip.


The impact of LUT Kyyti is not only environmental. Sharing commutes can also help colleagues from different departments, campuses, and functions get to know each other. In this way, LUT Kyyti can support a stronger sense of community while contributing to LUT’s climate goals.


Every shared ride is a small step, but together these choices can make a meaningful difference. By offering a seat, joining a colleague’s ride, or simply checking whether someone is travelling the same way, LUT and LAB community members can help reduce commuting emissions and make everyday travel more connected.
